# Nekhros, the First Risen — Commander Decklist

**Commander:** Nekhros, the First Risen — {4}{W}{U}{B}{R}{G}
*Legendary Creature — Zombie Avatar (Mythic)*
Eminence — As long as Nekhros is in the command zone or on the battlefield, Zombie spells you cast cost {1} less.
Menace
Whenever one or more Zombies you control attack, each opponent loses X life, where X is the number of attacking Zombies. You gain life equal to the total life lost this way.
10/10

**Thesis:** Flood the board with Zombies, multiply everything, drain the table.
**Total: 100 cards (1 commander + 99)**

## Game plan

**Early (turns 1–4):** You don't need Nekhros on the board — his eminence discounts every Zombie from the command zone from turn zero. Play lords and cheap Zombies at a discount (Undead Warchief stacks with eminence for {2} off), drop Burgeoning/Exploration, and set up draw (Rhystic Study, Phyrexian Arena). Gravecrawler + Carrion Feeder is your early engine: recursive body, free sac outlet, growing threat.

**Mid (turns 4–7):** Start attacking. Every Zombie that attacks drains each opponent — with 6 attackers that's 18 life leaving the table and 18 coming back to you, which is both your win condition *and* your Siphon/Citadel fuel. This is when the multipliers land: Anointed Procession, Reflections of Littjara, Endless Ranks. Veyla reanimates your fallen mid-combat; Ghorvath eats what's left and grows the Amalgam.

**Late (turns 7+):** Assemble the kill. Wound Reflection turns every drain trigger into double. Bolas's Citadel lets you play Zombies off the top of your library as long as your life total holds — and with Nekhros draining the table every combat, it holds. Gray Merchant or a big Shepherd of Rot activation closes games Wound Reflection has softened.

**Mulligan guidance:** Keep any hand with 3+ lands covering at least three colors, plus one of: a turn-1/2 Zombie (Gravecrawler, Carrion Feeder, Cryptbreaker), a draw engine (Rhystic Study, Phyrexian Arena), or ramp (Sol Ring, Burgeoning, Exploration). Mulligan aggressively away from hands that are all expensive cards — this deck wins by going wide early, not by casting Nekhros on turn 9 (though a 10/10 with menace draining the table is a fine plan B). With eminence live from the command zone, a hand of cheap Zombies + lands is always keepable.
