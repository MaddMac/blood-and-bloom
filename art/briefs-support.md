# BLOOD AND BLOOM — Art Briefs: Supporting Pieces

First-pass art direction for the eight supporting cards. These are briefs, not canon — reimagine freely. Empire pieces: gothic undead Rome (blackened steel, crimson, bone, blood-glow). Bloom pieces: petrified forest (pale stone, moss, brass clockwork roots, golden ichor light). All: dark painterly high-fantasy matte painting, portrait orientation, full-bleed illustration, no text or card frame.

## Empire

**1. Sanguine Membrane** — A living crimson membrane spreads like infection across a pale, stone-gray rootbound victim — the Bloom's insulation being colonized from within. Luminous veins of light thread through the translucent red film; tendrils creep over bark-like petrified skin. Mood: clinical horror, beautiful and wrong. Palette: arterial red against ash-gray, hot light in the veins. Composition: close on the victim's torso and face, half-consumed, dark ruinous background with a faint blood-glow.

**2. Membranous Infestation** — The infection at battlefield scale: a crimson membrane blooming outward over broken ground and toppled banners while ranks of undead zombies advance behind the spreading red tide. Ash drifts; blood-glow mist hangs low. Mood: an advancing front, inevitable. Palette: crimson, blackened steel, bone-white banners. Composition: low horizon, the red spread dominating the foreground, the legion emerging from the mist behind it.

**3. Exsanguinate the Fallen** — A zombie rises from a dark pool of blood in the foreground, crimson droplets suspended mid-air, while shadowy armored figures collapse and dissolve behind it — visibly drained, red wisps streaming from their bodies into the rising figure. Mood: theft made visible. Palette: deep reds, near-black shadows, bone highlights. Composition: the risen figure large and central, the collapsing figures receding into darkness.

**4. Legion Sanguimancer** — An undead wizard in tattered Roman legion robes, gaunt skeletal face, channels spiraling blood-wisps between raised clawed hands; the crimson spirals coil upward like a helix of smoke and light. Blackened steel ruins behind. Mood: disciplined power, ritual precision. Palette: crimson magic against charcoal darks, bone-white face. Composition: three-quarter figure, hands the focal point, spirals leading the eye upward.

**5. Decree of the Lifeless** — An undead herald in ceremonial robes reads from a bone scroll atop a tribunal dais while zombies seize struggling living soldiers around it — an execution ordered by paperwork. Gothic undead Roman architecture looms. Mood: cold bureaucracy of death. Palette: parchment bone, crimson seals and banners, steel grays. Composition: the herald elevated and central, the scroll catching the light, the struggle ringed around the dais below.

## Bloom

**6. Ironbloom Warden** — A squat construct guardian of bark and brass stands watch among gray petrified statues of creatures — the forest's own dead, kept as monuments. Golden ichor glows through the seams of its body; moss softens the stone around it. Mood: patient, immovable, faintly mournful. Palette: pale stone gray, moss green, warm brass, golden ichor light. Composition: the Warden centered and grounded, statues receding into a misty petrified forest.

**7. Rootbound Tithe** — A toll-gate of interwoven roots and pale stone; a blood mage's crimson spell shatters and dissolves harmlessly against the barrier, red light breaking like water on rock. Golden ichor seeps through the gate's seams. Mood: quiet invulnerability. Palette: root-brown and stone-gray against a single burst of defeated crimson, gold accents. Composition: the gate spanning the frame, the dissolving spell caught mid-break on its surface.

**8. Petrify the Leylines** — Continental scale, seen from above: glowing golden leylines vein a dark landscape, and petrification spreads along them like frost — light turning to stone in real time, gray swallowing gold along branching rivers of light. Mood: a world being quietly switched off. Palette: gold against deep blue-black darks, the gray of the spread. Composition: high aerial vista, the leyline network the dominant graphic element, the petrification front visibly advancing along it.
