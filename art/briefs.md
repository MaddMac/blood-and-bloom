# Blood and Bloom — Complete Art Briefs

First-pass art direction for all 31 pieces, written for community artists who want to reimagine them. These are briefs, not canon — reinterpret freely in any style.

**Shared direction for every card illustration:** dark painterly high-fantasy matte painting, cinematic lighting, rich detail, full-bleed illustration only, portrait orientation, no text, no watermark, no card frame.

- **Empire aesthetic:** gothic undead Rome — blackened steel, crimson, bone, candlelight and crimson-glow.
- **Bloom aesthetic:** petrified forest — pale stone, verdant moss, brass clockwork roots, golden ichor light. No veins, no blood. The Bloom is a garden, not body horror.

---

## EMPIRE MAJORS

**1. The Sanguine Crucible** — Mood: ritual awe and industrial dread — the Empire's faith made into plumbing. Palette: black stone, deep crimson, hot red-gold glow against candlelit shadow. Composition: low angle looking up a black ziggurat to a colossal chalice-vat brimming with crimson light; undead priests on the steps channel glowing red streams upward from ritual bowls; towering dark cathedral columns frame the scene, candles scattered like embers.

**2. Awakening of the First Risen** — Mood: apocalyptic grandeur — the moment the world realizes the dead outnumber the living. Palette: red dusk sky, black silhouettes, ember-orange horizon. Composition: wide panoramic shot of a desert battlefield, undead legions emerging from cracked crimson earth among broken banners; on the far horizon, a colossal shadowy progenitor wreathed in mist, arms outstretched.

**3. Covenant of the Lifeless Empire** — Mood: cold ceremony — power negotiated between two kinds of hunger. Palette: marble grey, blackened steel, crimson wax and robe accents, candlelight. Composition: symmetrical two-shot — left, a gaunt imperator in black Roman plate, empty calculating gaze; right, a sanguine archon in crimson robes wreathed in red mist; between them a stone altar bearing a scroll sealed with a glowing crimson sigil; skeletal legionaries and black banners receding into a shadowed marble hall.

**4. Sanguine Exsanguination Seminar** — Mood: scholarly menace — a university where the subject is blood. Palette: dark stone, parchment tones, glowing crimson channels, candle-gold. Composition: top-down-angled view into a stone amphitheater; tiered seats of hooded undead scholars ring a central ritual dais; luminous crimson channels carved in the floor radiate outward; a robed lecturer gestures over arcane instruments at the center.

**5. The Final Tally** — Mood: bureaucratic horror — death as accounting. Palette: aged bone white, tarnished brass, ghostly pale-blue numerals, deep shadow. Composition: a towering clockwork engine of interlocking bone gears and brass dials dominates the frame; ghostly translucent numerals drift upward from its core like souls being counted; tiny undead clerks on scaffolds tend the mechanism; dark vaulted chamber behind.

**6. Edict of Stillness** — Mood: serene, absolute authority — law as a physical force. Palette: white-gold radiance against cold blue-grey shadow, spectral legion in desaturated steel. Composition: a floating imperial stone tablet radiates concentric white-gold wards; before it, a spectral undead legion is frozen mid-march — banners halted mid-ripple, one soldier's raised foot suspended; the ward-light is the only warm thing in the frame.

**7. Mortach, Titan of the Lifeless** — Mood: siege-engine majesty — a walking fortress that used to be a man. Palette: grave-forged dark steel, bone, battlefield dust, ember accents. Composition: extreme low angle on a colossal undead giant in massive grave-forged plate armor, balefire glowing in the helm's eye slits; at his feet, swarms of tiny undead surge forward; broken fortifications and smoke behind.

**8. Cruor Vat of the Imperator** — Mood: foundry ritual — power refined like steel. Palette: brass, bone, churning crimson, furnace-orange underlight. Composition: interior of a legion foundry; a great brass-and-bone ritual vessel glowing with crimson light at center, pipes and chains feeding it; luminous vapor rising and condensing above; undead foundry-workers tend valves along the walls; furnace glow from below.

## BLOOM MAJORS

**9. The Thousand Roots** — Mood: awe at geological scale — the Bloom as a world-engine, ancient and unbothered. Palette: deep root-browns and shadowed stone giving way to radiant golden rings; moss-green midtones. Composition: a colossal world-tree of interwoven petrified roots dominates the vertical frame, crown out of frame; ten glowing golden rings orbit it like planetary bands at varying tilts; tiny human silhouettes at the base sell the scale; light radiates from the rings, rim-lighting the root mass.

**10. Uprising of the Bloom** — Mood: violent emergence — the forest itself standing up. Palette: splintered greens and sunlit golds against dark canopy shadow; pale stone highlights on the titans. Composition: two or three giant plant-construct titans rising in the midground as the forest canopy breaks open above them; leaves and shafts of light rain down through the breach; low-ish viewpoint so the titans tower; brass clockwork joints catch the light.

**11. The Bloom Listens** — Mood: hushed, intent stillness — a predator that hunts by hearing. Palette: muted earth tones, dark soil, one warm golden glow rising from below. Composition: a vast ear-like flower of root and bark pressed low to the ground, filling the lower two-thirds; fine root hairs trembling along its rim; a soft golden glow of a land's mana rising from the soil beneath, backlighting the hairs. Keep it quiet — the power is in the listening.

**12. The Great Petrification** — Mood: inexorable, silent catastrophe — no explosion, just stillness spreading. Palette: cold pale calcifying white rolling over dark undead armor tones; moss-green creeping at the edges; a band of golden ichor light on the horizon. Composition: a diagonal wave-front divides the frame — front ranks already frozen as pale stone statues mid-march, ranks behind still advancing into it. The wave reads as light, not fire — calcification, not burning.

**13. Calcifex, Bloom Titan** — Mood: immovable appetite — a mountain that drinks. Palette: weathered stone grays, deep moss greens, brass glints, golden ichor seeping through cracks. Composition: the titan fills the frame, three-quarter view, root tendrils extending outward and downward to drink from glowing tapped lands around his feet; the lands' glow contrasts with their dimmed, exhausted state. He should read as petrified wood and living stone — bark texture, stone weight.

**14. Sylvos, Bloom Egregore** — Mood: many minds, one will — serene and unsettling at once. Palette: verdant greens and pale stone, brass clockwork accents, one radiant golden bloom of psychic light at the crown. Composition: multiple moss-covered plant bodies and clockwork forms fused into a single towering egregore, symmetrical and totemic; at its crown, a shared bloom of golden light radiates outward like a thought made visible. The light is the focal point — everything converges on it.

**15. Harvest of the Fallen** — Mood: solemn, almost gentle — death as a harvest, not a slaughter. Palette: dusk purples and ember oranges in the sky; golden spirit-seeds as the brightest elements; dark battlefield below. Composition: a reaped battlefield at dusk in the lower third; spirits of the slain rising as glowing golden seeds, drawn upward along curving paths into waiting root-hands reaching down from a petrified canopy overhead. Vertical motion — everything ascends.

**16. Deep Roots Reclaim** — Mood: patient inevitability — nature doesn't hurry, and it never loses. Palette: golden dusk light on pale stone ruins; aggressive greens of new shoots; dark root masses in shadow. Composition: massive petrified roots splitting a ruined imperial fortress — cracked walls, toppled columns — with green shoots visibly splitting stone in the foreground; brass clockwork root-work threads through the rubble. The fortress should still read as imperial (order, geometry) so the contrast with the organic reclamation lands.

## SUPPORTING PIECES

**17. Sanguine Membrane** — Mood: clinical horror, beautiful and wrong — the Bloom's insulation being colonized from within. Palette: arterial red against ash-gray, hot light in the veins. Composition: close on a pale, stone-gray petrified figure's torso and face, half-covered by a living translucent crimson film; luminous threads weave through it; tendrils creep over bark-like skin; dark ruinous background with a faint red glow.

**18. Membranous Infestation** — Mood: an advancing front, inevitable. Palette: crimson, blackened steel, bone-white banners. Composition: low horizon; a tide of crimson mist blooming outward over broken ground and toppled banners dominates the foreground; ranks of undead legionnaires emerge from the mist behind it; ash drifts.

**19. Exsanguinate the Fallen** — Mood: theft made visible. Palette: deep reds, near-black shadows, bone highlights. Composition: an undead warrior rising amid pale golden light in the foreground, large and central; shadowy armored figures kneel and dissolve behind it, pale wisps streaming from their bodies into the rising figure; darkness recedes behind.

**20. Legion Sanguimancer** — Mood: disciplined power, ritual precision. Palette: crimson magic against charcoal darks, bone-white face. Composition: three-quarter figure of an undead wizard in tattered Roman legion robes, gaunt skeletal face; spiraling crimson wisps of light coil between raised clawed hands like a helix of smoke; blackened steel ruins behind; the hands are the focal point.

**21. Decree of the Lifeless** — Mood: the cold bureaucracy of death — an execution ordered by paperwork. Palette: parchment bone, crimson seals and banners, steel grays. Composition: an undead herald in ceremonial robes reads from a bone scroll atop a tribunal dais, elevated and central, the scroll catching the light; undead soldiers seize struggling living captives ringed around the dais below; gothic undead Roman architecture looms.

**22. Ironbloom Warden** — Mood: patient, immovable, faintly mournful. Palette: pale stone gray, moss green, warm brass, golden ichor light. Composition: a squat guardian construct of bark and brass stands watch, centered and grounded, among gray petrified statues; golden ichor glows through its seams; moss softens the stone; statues recede into a misty petrified forest.

**23. Rootbound Tithe** — Mood: quiet invulnerability. Palette: root-brown and stone-gray against a single burst of defeated crimson, gold accents. Composition: a toll-gate of interwoven roots and pale stone spans the frame; a bolt of crimson spell-light shatters and dissolves harmlessly against it, caught mid-break; golden ichor seeps through the gate's seams.

**24. Petrify the Leylines** — Mood: a world being quietly switched off. Palette: gold against deep blue-black darks, the gray of the spreading petrification. Composition: high aerial vista — glowing golden leylines vein a dark landscape, and petrification spreads along them like frost; gray visibly swallows gold along branching rivers of light; the leyline network is the dominant graphic element.

## COMMONS AND SET SYMBOL

**25. Drain the Defiant** — Mood: intimate cruelty — one man's defiance, measured out. Palette: dungeon darks, steel gray, a single thread of pale light, bone-white vial. Composition: tight shot — a defiant soldier on his knees, pale light streaming from him into a floating crystal vial held by an unseen force; the vial glows brighter than anything else in the frame; darkness presses in.

**26. Tides of Cruor** — Mood: the battlefield itself turning against the living. Palette: crimson mist, ember orange, blackened steel, bone. Composition: a tide of crimson mist sweeps across a battlefield at dusk, carrying broken banners and debris; tiny figures flee or stand against it; the mist is the protagonist — vast, low, unstoppable.

**27. Petrified Rebuke** — Mood: sudden, total negation. Palette: one violent burst of crimson against pale blooming stone and moss green. Composition: a bolt of crimson spell-light shatters mid-flight against a wall of pale stone that blooms with carved flowers; fragments of the spell hang frozen in the air; the stone is utterly unmoved.

**28. Drag Them Down** — Mood: the sky is not safe either. Palette: storm gray and bruised purple, vivid green vines, one struggling winged silhouette. Composition: thick vines erupt upward into a stormy sky, coiling around a winged creature and yanking it down; feathers and leaves scatter; the ground below is a dark petrified forest reaching up.

**29. Ironbloom Sapling** — Mood: sinister patience — the Bloom can wait longer than you can. Palette: moonlit silver-green, deep garden shadow, one exhausted warm figure. Composition: a small sapling unfurls its leaves over a sleeping, exhausted soldier; moonlight through a canopy; the sapling is small but centered and inevitable; the soldier doesn't know.

**30. Ironbloom Deep-Rooter** — Mood: the war beneath the war. Palette: earthy dark browns, golden root-light, dim battlefield above. Composition: cross-section view — a massive root system spreads beneath a battlefield like an inverted tree; tiny soldiers and banners dot the surface strip at top for scale; a few roots glow faintly gold where they drink.

**31. Set symbol** — A simple iconic emblem: a single droplet merged with a blooming flower, solid black silhouette on white, minimalist high-contrast logo style, no background detail. Must read at card-symbol size: bold shapes, no fine lines.
