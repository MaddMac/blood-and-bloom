# Art Briefs — Empire Majors (community reimagining briefs)

One paragraph per piece: mood, palette, composition. These describe first-pass proxy direction only — reimagine freely in any style.

## 1. The Sanguine Crucible
Mood: ritual awe and industrial dread — the Empire's faith made into plumbing. Palette: black stone, deep crimson, hot red-gold blood-glow against candlelit shadow. Composition: low angle looking up a black ziggurat to a colossal chalice-vat brimming with blood; undead priests on the steps channel glowing red streams upward from ritual bowls; towering dark cathedral columns frame the scene, candles scattered like embers.

## 2. Awakening of the First Risen
Mood: apocalyptic grandeur — the moment the world realizes the dead outnumber the living. Palette: blood-red dusk sky, black silhouettes, ember-orange horizon. Composition: wide panoramic shot of a desert battlefield, zombie legions clawing up from cracked crimson earth among broken banners; on the far horizon, a colossal shadowy progenitor wreathed in mist, arms outstretched, commanding the rising.

## 3. Covenant of the Lifeless Empire
Mood: cold ceremony — power negotiated between two kinds of hunger. Palette: marble grey, blackened steel, crimson wax and robe accents, candlelight. Composition: symmetrical two-shot — left, a gaunt imperator in black Roman plate, empty calculating gaze; right, a sanguine archon in crimson robes wreathed in blood-mist; between them a stone altar bearing a scroll sealed with a glowing blood-red sigil; skeletal legionaries and black banners recede into a shadowed marble hall.

## 4. Sanguine Exsanguination Seminar
Mood: scholarly menace — a university where the subject is blood. Palette: dark stone, parchment tones, glowing crimson channels, candle-gold. Composition: top-down-angled view into a stone amphitheater; tiered seats of hooded undead scholars ring a central slab; luminous blood channels carved in the floor radiate outward like veins; a robed lecturer gestures over arcane instruments at the center.

## 5. The Final Tally
Mood: bureaucratic horror — death as accounting. Palette: aged bone white, tarnished brass, ghostly pale-blue numerals, deep shadow. Composition: a towering clockwork engine of interlocking bone gears and brass dials dominates the frame; ghostly translucent numerals drift upward from its core like souls being counted; tiny undead clerks on scaffolds tend the mechanism; dark vaulted chamber behind.

## 6. Edict of Stillness
Mood: serene, absolute authority — law as a physical force. Palette: white-gold radiance against cold blue-grey shadow, spectral legion in desaturated steel. Composition: a floating imperial stone tablet radiates concentric white-gold wards; before it, a spectral undead legion is frozen mid-march — banners halted mid-ripple, one soldier's raised foot suspended; the ward-light is the only warm thing in the frame.

## 7. Mortach, Titan of the Lifeless
Mood: siege-engine majesty — a walking fortress that used to be a man. Palette: grave-forged dark steel, bone, battlefield dust, ember accents. Composition: extreme low angle on a colossal zombie giant in massive grave-forged plate armor, deathtouch-green balefire in the helm's eye slits; at his feet, swarms of tiny zombie tokens surge forward like a tide; broken fortifications and smoke behind.

## 8. Cruor Vat of the Imperator
Mood: foundry ritual — blood refined like steel. Palette: brass, bone, churning crimson, furnace-orange underlight. Composition: interior of a legion foundry; a great brass-and-bone vat of churning blood at center, pipes and chains feeding it; above the vat, glowing red Blood tokens condense out of the vapor like droplets; undead foundry-workers tend valves along the walls; furnace glow from below.
