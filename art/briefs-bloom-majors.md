# Art Briefs — Bloom Major Pieces

Direction for community artists reimagining these cards. All pieces share the Ironbloom Conservatory aesthetic: petrified forest — pale stone, verdant moss, brass clockwork roots, golden ichor light. No veins, no blood. The Bloom is a garden, not body horror. Dark painterly high-fantasy matte painting; portrait orientation; full-bleed illustration, no text or frames.

## 1. The Thousand Roots
**Mood:** Awe at geological scale — the Bloom as a world-engine, ancient and unbothered. **Palette:** Deep root-browns and shadowed stone giving way to radiant golden rings; moss-green midtones. **Composition:** A colossal world-tree of interwoven petrified roots dominates the vertical frame, its crown out of frame; ten glowing golden rings orbit it like planetary bands at varying tilts; tiny human silhouettes at the base sell the scale. Light radiates from the rings, rim-lighting the root mass.

## 2. Uprising of the Bloom
**Mood:** Violent emergence — the forest itself standing up. **Palette:** Splintered greens and sunlit golds against dark canopy shadow; pale stone highlights on the titans. **Composition:** Two or three giant plant-construct titans rising in the midground as the forest canopy breaks open above them; leaves and shafts of light rain down through the breach. Low-ish viewpoint so the titans tower; their brass clockwork joints catch the light.

## 3. The Bloom Listens
**Mood:** Hushed, intent stillness — a predator that hunts by hearing. **Palette:** Muted earth tones, dark soil, one warm golden glow rising from below. **Composition:** A vast ear-like flower of root and bark pressed low to the ground, filling the lower two-thirds; fine root hairs trembling along its rim; a soft golden glow of a land's mana rising from the soil beneath it, backlighting the hairs. Keep it quiet — the power is in the listening.

## 4. The Great Petrification
**Mood:** Inexorable, silent catastrophe — no explosion, just stillness spreading. **Palette:** Cold pale calcifying white rolling over dark undead armor tones; moss-green creeping at the edges; a band of golden ichor light on the horizon. **Composition:** A diagonal wave-front divides the frame: front ranks already frozen as pale stone statues mid-march, ranks behind still advancing into it. The wave reads as light, not fire — calcification, not burning.

## 5. Calcifex, Bloom Titan
**Mood:** Immovable appetite — a mountain that drinks. **Palette:** Weathered stone grays, deep moss greens, brass glints, golden ichor seeping through cracks. **Composition:** The titan fills the frame, three-quarter view, root tendrils extending outward and downward to drink from glowing tapped lands around his feet; the lands' glow contrasts with their dimmed, exhausted state. He should read as petrified wood and living stone — bark texture, stone weight.

## 6. Sylvos, Bloom Egregore
**Mood:** Many minds, one will — serene and unsettling at once. **Palette:** Verdant greens and pale stone, brass clockwork accents, one radiant golden bloom of psychic light at the crown. **Composition:** Multiple moss-covered plant bodies and clockwork forms fused into a single towering egregore, symmetrical-ish and totemic; at its crown, a shared bloom of golden light radiates outward like a thought made visible. The light is the focal point — everything converges on it.

## 7. Harvest of the Fallen
**Mood:** Solemn, almost gentle — death as a harvest, not a slaughter. **Palette:** Dusk purples and ember oranges in the sky; golden spirit-seeds as the brightest elements; dark battlefield below. **Composition:** A reaped battlefield at dusk in the lower third; spirits of the slain rising as glowing golden seeds, drawn upward along curving paths into waiting root-hands reaching down from a petrified canopy overhead. Vertical motion — everything ascends.

## 8. Deep Roots Reclaim
**Mood:** Patient inevitability — nature doesn't hurry, and it never loses. **Palette:** Golden dusk light on pale stone ruins; aggressive greens of new shoots; dark root masses in shadow. **Composition:** Massive petrified roots splitting a ruined imperial fortress — cracked walls, toppled columns — with green shoots visibly splitting stone in the foreground; brass clockwork root-work threads through the rubble. The fortress should still read as imperial (order, geometry) so the contrast with the organic reclamation lands.
