# Blood and Bloom

### *Two hungers. One world.*

A custom Magic: The Gathering plane and the set built on it — open-source, proxy-friendly, and built to be broken.

Two factions wage war over the world's lifeblood. **The Lifeless Empire** practices **hemomancy**: disciplined undead whose spells get cheaper off life their enemies have already lost this turn (**Siphon**). **The Ironbloom Conservatory** is a veinless plant/construct hive mind that **petrifies** what it touches — creatures and lands with petrification counters can't attack, block, or untap — and its people are **Rootbound**, immune to Siphon entirely. The Empire's answer is the **Sanguine Membrane**, which breaks Rootbound and makes the victim's life count double.

Every weapon has a mirror answer. That's the design thesis.

## Play it tonight

1. Import `decklists/nekhros-commander-moxfield.txt` into Moxfield (bulk import), or proxy from the decklist.
2. The 6 custom cards (Nekhros + the five aspects) need proxying: print the art, sleeve over bulk. On Moxfield, add them as custom cards manually.
3. Read the playtest manual's faction primer for your deck (5 minutes).
4. Play 3+ games, answer the five feedback questions, file break reports as issues.

## Contribute

- **Break it.** Named bounties in the playtest manual: break Siphon, break Paradigm, break Harvest of the Fallen, break Vorthax, break the Final Tally. Exact sequences only — "turn 6 with X, Y, Z does this" is data.
- **Reimagine the art.** All current art is first-pass proxy art — direction, not canon. Original reimaginings welcome; the strongest get curated into the gallery with credit. Per-card briefs in `art/`.
- **Ground rule:** preserve each faction's core fantasy. Hemomancy *must* feed on the opponent's spilled life; the Bloom *must* stay veinless and patient. Fix balance problems *around* the fantasy, never by replacing it.
